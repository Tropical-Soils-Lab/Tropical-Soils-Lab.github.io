Our laboratory

Soils connect agriculture,
ecosystems, and people.

At the University of Florida, our laboratory brings soil science into the decisions that shape agricultural systems. Our focus spans soil health assessment, sustainable management, and practical digital tools.

Dr. Márcio R. Nunes directs the laboratory within the Department of Soil, Water, and Ecosystem Sciences, UF/IFAS.
