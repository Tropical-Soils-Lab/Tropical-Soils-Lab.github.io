Márcio R. Nunes

Director of the Sustainable Tropical Soil Management Laboratory · Assistant Professor of Tropical Soils, UF/IFAS.

Lab director

From a family farm
to soil science.

Research · Teaching · Soil health

Raised on a farm in southern Brazil, Márcio brings agricultural experience to research on soil management in tropical and temperate environments. His work connects soil health assessment with agricultural productivity and natural resource stewardship.
