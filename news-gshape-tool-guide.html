Meet gSHAPE: soil health assessment in your browser

Our tool guide introduces the interface, available indicator groups, and steps to get started.

gSHAPE is a web tool for context-specific soil health assessment and benchmarking. Its start screen brings together location setup, evaluation framework selection, and physical, chemical, and biological indicators.

The new guide explains how to choose a location, select an available framework, identify measured indicators, and follow the requested inputs and units. A screenshot shows the real interface before an assessment is entered.

The guide links directly to the live app. The screenshot is an interface example and does not represent an assessment result.
