SHAPEv1.0: scoring curves and peer-group benchmarks

A publication in Soil Science Society of America Journal advances soil health interpretation in context.

‘SHAPEv1.0 Scoring curves and peer group benchmarks for dynamic soil health indicators’ describes an approach for interpreting soil measurements using site-specific peer groups.

The work forms part of Dr. Márcio R. Nunes’s research portfolio on soil health scoring and benchmarking. It builds on the SHAPE framework introduced for soil organic carbon.

Visit the original publication to explore the scoring approach and supporting research.
