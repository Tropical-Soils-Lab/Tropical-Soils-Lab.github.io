A region-specific soil health framework for Sub-Saharan Africa

A study by Biru, Nunes, and collaborators appears in Communications Earth & Environment.

The paper ‘A region-specific soil health assessment protocol and evaluation for Sub-Saharan Africa’ presents SHAPE-SSA, a regional approach to soil health interpretation.

The author team includes Moges Kidane Biru, Marcio R. Nunes, and collaborators. The publication extends the lab’s interest in interpreting soil measurements within their environmental context.

Read the original paper for the framework, data, methods, and results.
