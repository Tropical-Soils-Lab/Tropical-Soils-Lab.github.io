Assessing SMAF transferability in tropical agroecosystems

Research with Mohkam Singh and Márcio R. Nunes examines soil management assessment across environments.

Amorim, Ashworth, Drescher, Singh, and Nunes published ‘Transferability of soil management assessment framework indices to detect best soil management strategies in tropical agroecosystems’ in Agricultural & Environmental Letters.

The study examines whether soil health scoring approaches can detect management differences across contrasting environments. It contributes to the question of how assessment frameworks should be applied in tropical agriculture.

The publisher’s article provides the study design and findings.
